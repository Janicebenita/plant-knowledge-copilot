# SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — Maintenance triage policy

This fictional policy from the supplied starter illustrates retrieval and citation behavior only. Plant-specific operating limits, isolation steps, and escalation rules must come from approved site documents.

## Triage

Record the asset ID, observation time, instrument units, and operator notes. Compare measurements with the asset's approved limits and historical trend. Escalate an active leak to the site maintenance supervisor for assessment. A single measurement does not prove failure mechanism.

## Documentation

Link work orders and inspection findings to their source records. If evidence is missing, record the gap and ask the responsible engineer to verify it. Do not present a diagnosis or safety instruction as approved merely because it appears in an AI answer.
